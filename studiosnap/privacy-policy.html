Privacy Policy

StudioSnap · Last updated: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how StudioSnap ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application ("the App"). The App turns photos of your clothes into listing photos, on a model or on a backdrop. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

1. Information We Collect

Photos of your items

When you make photos, the photos of your items you choose are sent securely to our AI processing service (Replicate) to create your listing photos. We do not keep these photos, or the photos made from them, on our servers once processing has finished. Finished photos are saved on your device.

Your own model photo

If you choose to use a photo of yourself as the model, that photo is stored in our cloud storage (Google Firebase), linked to your account, so you can use it again. It stays there until you delete it in the App or delete your account.

Account information

To make photos and buy credits, you sign in with Google or Apple. We receive a user identifier, your email address and, if you share it, your name from these providers to create and manage your account. We do not receive your password.

Purchase information

Credit purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We do not process or store your payment card details. We receive transaction records (for example, which credit pack was bought) to add credits to your account.

Usage data and analytics

We collect usage data through Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used and to improve it, such as app opens, photos made and store views. Firebase Analytics uses an app instance identifier and, on Android, may collect the device's advertising ID. We do not show ads and we do not use this data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Crash reports

If the App crashes, we collect crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix bugs. Crash reports include the device model, operating system version, a technical record of the error and your account's user identifier.

History

A record of the photos you've made (file references and dates) is kept in a database on your device. It does not leave your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To make your listing photos and return them to you
  • To manage your account and credit balance
  • To complete in-app purchases
  • To improve the App through analytics
  • To find and fix technical problems through crash reports

We do not use your photos to train AI models, and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Sharing of Information

We share information only with the following service providers, as needed to run the App:

  • Replicate – AI image processing. Photos are sent for processing only.
  • RevenueCat – In-app purchase and credit management.
  • Google Firebase – Sign-in, cloud storage of your own model photo, analytics and crash reporting.
  • Apple / Google – Sign-in and payment processing on their platforms.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your account data is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the App: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your credit balance, your stored model photo and your purchase records with RevenueCat, and on iOS disconnects Sign in with Apple. Photos saved on your device and your History stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Purchase records held by Apple or Google are kept by them. More detail: Delete your account.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and we take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will show significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
