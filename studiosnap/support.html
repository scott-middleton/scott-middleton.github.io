Frequently Asked Questions

What does StudioSnap do?

It turns a photo of your clothing into listing photos: your item worn by a model, or on its own on a clean backdrop. Make a whole listing (front, back and the item on its own, with the same model in the same room) or quick photos on a ready-made scene.

How do credits work?

Each photo costs 1 or 2 credits, and the App shows the cost before you make anything. Buy credit packs in the App's store. Credits don't expire. New sellers get free credits to start.

What if a photo fails?

Credits for a photo that fails are refunded to your account automatically.

My credits haven't appeared after buying

Make sure you're signed in with the same Google or Apple account you bought with, then close and reopen the App. If they still haven't appeared after a few minutes, email us with your purchase receipt and we'll sort it out.

Can I use a photo of myself as the model?

Yes. Your photo is stored with your account so you can use it again, until you delete it or your account.

Are my item photos kept on your servers?

No. They're sent securely for processing and not kept once your photos are made. Finished photos are saved on your phone. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I get a refund on a purchase?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so purchase refunds are requested through them (reportaproblem.apple.com on iPhone, or Google Play's order history on Android). If you think credits were taken by mistake, email us and we'll look into it.

How do I delete my account?

In the App: Settings → Delete Account. It permanently deletes your account and its data, including any remaining credits. More detail: Delete your account.
